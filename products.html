Common Questions

Is this another baby registry checklist?

No. The Baby Registry Auditor doesn't tell you what every parent should buy. It helps you evaluate what makes sense for your family.

What exactly am I getting

A Google Sheets-based decision framework that helps you evaluate registry items, organize purchases, and avoid unnecessary spending.

What makes it different from registry guides online?

Most registry guides tell you what they recommend. The Auditor gives you a process for deciding what you actually need.

Will I save money?

Most parents will likely avoid unnecessary purchases, duplicate items, and products they don't need right away, but the biggest benefit is making calmer, more intentional decisions.

Why isn't there a launch date yet?

I'm still refining the tool to make sure it's genuinely useful. Waitlist members will be the first to hear when it's ready.
